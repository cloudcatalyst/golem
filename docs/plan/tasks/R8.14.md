## Goal

`golem ext` shipped **read-only** on purpose (Decision 53(e)): `list` / `status` with
spawn-free, `PATHEXT`-aware detection. This is the deliberately-deferred write half —
`install`, `remove`, `upgrade`.

## Why it was deferred, and what that implies

The read surface answered the question that prompted the work ("is Headroom actually
running?" — no, and the `gate` field said why). Installing is a different risk class:
it fetches and executes third-party code on the user's machine.

So the design constraint is fixed in advance by Decision 53: **Golem distributes no
third-party bytes.** `install` must invoke *the upstream's own installer* at a pinned
version, with explicit consent — never vendor, never mirror, never auto-download on
first use. A binary is permissible as a *spawn target* and forbidden as *cargo*.

## Hard constraints

- Consent is explicit and per-tool. Installing is an outward, machine-modifying act:
  it goes through the autonomy gate (ADR-0002) and is never implied by `list`.
- Pinned versions only, recorded in the manifest beside the existing `tier` / `detect` /
  `gate` fields.
- Cross-platform: argument-array spawn, `PATHEXT` on Windows, no shell strings.
- `upgrade` must not be able to move a pin outside its playbook. The Headroom pin
  specifically is governed by the T-C4 upgrade playbook, and CLAUDE.md forbids bumping
  it outside that (`src/compression/index.ts`).
- The existing drift guards stay green: `enabledBy` paths asserted against
  `SETTINGS_LEAVES`, `requires` ids against the registry, adapters against the
  filesystem, criterion 4 against `package.json`'s mandatory `dependencies`.

## Out of scope

A package manager. A registry service. Installing anything that fails Decision 53's
four-criterion admission bar.
