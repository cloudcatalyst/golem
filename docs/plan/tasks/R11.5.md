## What was wrong

`WIKI.md`'s Index is the entry point the wiki-first rule tells every agent to
skim before searching. The close-out checklist says to author a debrief. Nothing
tied the two together — so of 121 debriefs, **39 had no Index line**: every one
from 2026-07-16 onward, including all of R9 and R10.

The pages were not lost. The vector index still found them, because it covers the
tree. What they had fallen out of was the **graph**: an agent starting at the
Index, following wikilinks, could not reach seven weeks of debriefs. That is the
half the wiki exists for — a raw capture is searchable but disconnected, and a
page nothing links to is exactly that.

The reason it went unnoticed is that `golem wiki check` passed the whole time. It
lints what a page *contains* (frontmatter, dates, resolvable wikilinks) and had
nothing to say about whether anything points *at* the page.

## What was done

1. All 39 backfilled, in date order, each with a one-line hook read from the
   debrief itself rather than generated from its title.
2. `checkWiki` gained one rule: a page under `debriefs/` whose path the
   `type: schema` page (`WIKI.md`) does not mention is an issue.

Scoped to `debriefs/` on purpose. Those are append-only records whose whole value
is being reachable later. Concept pages are linked from each other and from the
zone sections, so demanding an Index line for every one of them would be noise
rather than a check.

## Out of scope

- Enforcing the *quality* of a hook. A line that says nothing passes; that is a
  review question, not a lint.
- The other zones (`syntheses/`, `sources/`, `questions/`). None showed drift,
  and each has a different reachability story.
- Auto-fixing. `wiki check` is read-only by design (WS-W W2) — it reports, a
  human or an approved write fixes.

## Gate

Standing verification bar. Two new `wiki.test.ts` cases: an unlisted debrief is
flagged (and the listed one beside it is not), and a concept page with no Index
line is NOT flagged. Plus `golem wiki check` clean on this repo's own 158 pages.

## Outcome

shipped — 121 of 121 debriefs indexed, and `golem wiki check` now fails on an
unlisted one. Found while auditing what the close-out checklist actually produced
versus what it claims.
