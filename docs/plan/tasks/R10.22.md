## How it surfaced

R10.21's `golem init` run reported eight `conflict` lines — files Golem would
have updated but refused to overwrite because they had been edited. Asked whether
anything in them needed folding back, the diff split cleanly in two:

* **Four skills** (`slider`, `expand`, `bypass`, `triage`) were STALE ON DISK —
  the shipped text was newer in every case (the `golem:layering-exception`
  comments, and MCP-tool names where the disk copies still said `golem stats` /
  `golem devices`). Nothing to fold back; deleted and re-inited.
* **Four rules** were the opposite: hand-condensed rewrites, ~55% shorter, of
  text Golem ships.

## What the rules actually contained

Checked fact by fact against the shipped snippets. Exactly one piece of new
information — *"ADRs go in `docs/decisions/`, not the wiki"* — and four
load-bearing facts DROPPED by the condensation:

* the cold-feed warning in `snooze-hold`: when an account's responses carry no
  rate-limit headers, the auto-park is **blind**, and the human has to watch
  Claude Code's own indicator and park manually;
* `knowledge.wiki_dir` is configurable — the wiki is not always `docs/wiki/`;
* redaction-before-storage still applies, and contradictions are surfaced to the
  human rather than auto-resolved;
* local-answer is single-turn only — never mid-conversation, never on tool-use
  turns.

## Why the size mattered enough to act on

Seeded rules load into **every** session. The five `seededByDefault` snippets
totalled 8,322 bytes of permanent context in a project whose entire thesis is
token economy — Golem was spending, on every conversation, exactly the kind of
budget it exists to defend. The rewrite lands at 5,684 bytes (−32%) with the four
facts above restored.

## The one place brevity was refused

Three assertions in `tests/integration/hooks/guidance.test.ts` pin exact phrases:
the wiki ladder's ordering (`Check the wiki first` → `` `search` MCP tool `` →
`Then WebFetch`), `keep the wiki current`, and `ENFORCES` for the PreToolUse
gate. Those encode contract, not wording — the phrasing was restored rather than
the tests loosened.

## Note for the next person

A deleted default rule stays deleted: `golem init` seeds behind a sentinel so
that disabling a default sticks across re-inits (by design). Refreshing one is
`golem guidance enable <name>`, not `golem init`.
