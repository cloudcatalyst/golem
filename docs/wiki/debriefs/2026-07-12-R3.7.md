# R3.7 — LanceDB scale driver spike

**Before:** ROADMAP flagged LanceDB as "optional; only pays at 10⁵+
vectors," but nothing had actually measured `FileVectorDriver`
(`src/knowledge/file-driver.ts`) against real or extrapolated scale to
confirm that number, or check whether it degrades gracefully as it
approaches it.

## What was measured

A throwaway benchmark (not committed — R3.7 is a read-only measurement
task) drove `FileVectorDriver` at the real on-disk chunk count (3,967
across two collections, from `.golem/knowledge/*/meta.json`) and at
synthetic 4k/10k/30k/50k/100k-chunk scales, timing batched ingest,
`search(k=10)`, and steady-state single-chunk `upsert`.

## Finding

Full detail in [[r3.7-lancedb-scale-spike]]. Two things, both different from
what was expected:

1. **Search was never the problem.** Brute-force cosine stayed under 75ms
   even at 30,000 chunks — LanceDB's ANN index would solve a bottleneck that
   doesn't exist yet.
2. **The real cost is `#flush()` rewriting the entire collection on every
   upsert** (steady-state single-chunk ingest: 443ms at 4k chunks → 3.3s at
   30k), and it has a **hard crash wall between 30,000-50,000 chunks** —
   `RangeError: Invalid string length` from `Array.join`-ing the whole
   collection into one JS string before writing it. That's earlier than
   ROADMAP's assumed "10⁵" figure, though still ~8-13x above today's real
   usage (3,967 chunks).

## Go/no-go

**No-go on LanceDB today** — real usage is nowhere near either the crash
wall or the originally-assumed threshold. **But recorded a concrete, cheaper
near-term fix**: stream-write `#flush()` instead of `Array.join`, which
removes the crash ceiling and the O(corpus-size) steady-state ingest cost in
one pure-TS change, no new dependency. Not built here (out of this spike's
read-only scope, same pattern as [[R2.5 — Headroom read_lifecycle disable verification]]
recommending R2.6's shape without building it in the spike
itself) — left as a follow-up recommendation.

## Verified

Measurement-only task; no source changes. `docs/plan/ROADMAP.md`'s R3.7 row
updated with the finding and recommendation.

See also [[r3.7-lancedb-scale-spike]].
